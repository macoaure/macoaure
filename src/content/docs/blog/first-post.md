This is the first note on my new personal site.

I wanted a home base that could hold three things without making any of them complicated: a resume, a blog, and a record of what I’m learning. Starlight gives me a fast, readable writing environment, while keeping the site easy to extend when it needs more shape.

For now, the goal is simple: publish useful things occasionally and keep the rest of the site quiet.

## What comes next

- Share notes from projects and experiments.
- Keep the resume current.
- Build only the pages that earn their place.
